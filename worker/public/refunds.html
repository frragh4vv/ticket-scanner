Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

What you're buying

TixDesk sells prepaid credit packs. One credit lets you generate one QR pass. Prices are in Indian Rupees (INR).

Delivery

Credits are digital and delivered instantly: they're added to your account as soon as Razorpay confirms the payment. Nothing is shipped. If credits don't appear within 15 minutes of a successful payment, contact us with your order ID.

Refunds

  • Unused packs: you can ask for a full refund within 7 days of purchase, as long as none of that pack's credits have been used.
  • Partly used packs: not refundable, except where required by law or when the Service failed because of our fault.
  • Failed or duplicate payments: if you were charged but credits weren't added, or you were charged twice, we refund the extra payment in full.
  • Free credits can't be exchanged for money.

How to ask for a refund

Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from your account's email address with your order ID (it starts with order_). We reply within 2 business days.

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay. They usually arrive within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank.

Cancellation

There's no subscription to cancel: you only pay when you buy a pack. You can delete your account at any time from Settings. Unused credits are forfeited when you delete your account, so request any refund first.

Refunds for your attendees

TixDesk doesn't sell tickets or hold attendee money. When you buy a ticket on a TixDesk booking page, you pay the event organizer directly through their own Razorpay account; TixDesk never receives that money, so we can't issue ticket refunds.

Refunds, cancellations and payment disputes for a ticket are the organizer's responsibility, under the organizer's own policy. Contact the organizer of your event. If you can't reach them, write to us and we'll pass your message on, but we can't decide or pay the refund.
