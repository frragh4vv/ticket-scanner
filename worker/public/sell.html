Sell tickets online

Buyers pay on your public booking page. The money goes straight to your own Razorpay account.

1. Connect your Razorpay

Before you change keys: buyers who are paying right now were sent to your current Razorpay account, so finish or wait out those payments first (about 15 minutes after the last checkout). Switching from Test to Live is fine. Switching to a different Razorpay account means new payments go there, and any webhook you set up must be added again in that account.

In Razorpay open Account & Settings → API Keys and generate a key. Paste both halves here. Start with a Test mode key to try a booking without real money, then switch to a Live key.

That's all the setup needed. If a buyer pays but closes the page before coming back, we check with Razorpay and still send their tickets, usually within 5 minutes.

Advanced: instant confirmation (optional)

Add a webhook in Razorpay to confirm those late payments in seconds instead of minutes. Skip this unless you're comfortable in the Razorpay dashboard: sales work fine without it.
