Who we are

TixDesk is a service for event organizers to design QR passes, send them to attendees and scan them at the door. TixDesk is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [REGISTERED ADDRESS] (“we”, “us”).

This policy explains what personal data we handle, why, and the choices you have. It is written for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules.

Our role for each kind of data

  • Organizer accounts: we decide how this data is used, so we are the data fiduciary.
  • Attendee details that organizers upload (names, emails, phone numbers, scan history): the organizer decides why it is collected and is the data fiduciary. We process it only on the organizer's instructions to issue, send and check passes, acting as their data processor. Attendees should contact the event organizer first; we will help organizers answer those requests.

Why we're allowed to use it

  • Consent you give when you create an account and accept these terms, which you can withdraw by deleting your account.
  • Legitimate uses allowed by the DPDP Act, such as complying with law (tax records) and responding to security incidents.
  • For attendee data, the organizer is responsible for having a lawful basis and for telling attendees how their data is used, for example on their ticket sales page.

Where your data is stored

Cloudflare runs a global network, and our database's primary copy may be located outside India. Data transfers follow the DPDP Act and any restrictions the Government of India notifies.

How long we keep it

  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Events, passes and scan history: until the organizer deletes them (Settings → Wipe records) or deletes their account.
  • Payment records: kept for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires (currently up to 8 years). After account deletion they are detached from your name and email.
  • Rate-limit records (IP, email or event used in sign-in attempts): overwritten within 24 hours.
  • Cloudflare request logs: kept according to Cloudflare's retention settings.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • Access your data: Settings → Account → Download my data gives you everything stored for your account as a JSON file.
  • Correct it: edit attendee details in the dashboard, or write to us about account details.
  • Erase it: Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your events, passes, attendee data and scan history.
  • Withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to.
  • Raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer (below), and, if you're not satisfied, with the Data Protection Board of India.

Attendees: please contact the organizer of your event first. If you can't reach them, write to us and we'll pass your request on.

How we protect it

  • All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS (TLS).
  • Passwords are salted and hashed with PBKDF2-SHA256; we can't read them.
  • Sessions are signed tokens that expire, and a password change signs out every device.
  • Each organizer can only reach their own events. Gate PINs unlock scanning for a single event and nothing else.
  • Repeated failed sign-ins and PIN guesses are blocked.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your personal data, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board as the DPDP Act requires.

Cookies and browser storage

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. See the Cookie Policy for the small amount of browser storage TixDesk needs.

Children

Organizer accounts are for people 18 and older. Organizers who issue passes to attendees under 18 must get verifiable consent from a parent or guardian, as the DPDP Act requires.
