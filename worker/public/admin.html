Help

Questions organizers ask

What's the order of things?

1. Create an event. 2. Generate blank tickets. 3. Design them in Ticket studio. 4. Put names on them (click a row, or Assign tickets for a list). 5. Email unsent on Home. 6. On the day, share the gate link and PIN with volunteers and scan.

What uses a credit?

Only generating a ticket: one credit each. Putting names on tickets, designing, emailing, scanning and exporting are free. You start with 50 free credits; packs are ₹10 a ticket (500), ₹8 (2,500) or ₹5 (10,000), and credits never expire.

Why are tickets made blank?

So you can generate once and fill names as sales come in. A blank ticket already has its QR code; adding a name just labels it.

I put the wrong name or email on a ticket.

Click the name or email in the Home list and type the right one; it saves by itself. If that ticket was already emailed, the old email still works for entry, so check before you resend it to someone else.

How do volunteers scan at the door?

Press Copy link next to the gate PIN and send it to them. They open it on their phone, enter the PIN, allow the camera and scan. They only see the scanner, never your guest list.

Can a ticket be used twice?

No. Every scan is checked live against one shared record, so a screenshot shown at a second gate is flagged as already used, with the time.

A guest didn't get their email.

Ask them to check spam, then resend from the mail button on their row. You can also download their ticket from Ticket studio and send it on WhatsApp.

Can I change an event after creating it?

No. The name, dates, tiers and gate PIN are fixed so tickets already sent stay valid. Create a new event if something is wrong.

Can I get a refund?

Unused credit packs are refundable within 7 days. See the refund policy.
