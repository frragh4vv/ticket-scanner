Cookie Policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

The short version

stub doesn't use advertising cookies, analytics or tracking pixels. The website and dashboard store a few items in your browser that the Service can't work without. Because they are strictly necessary, we show a notice rather than asking for consent.

What stub stores in your browser

stub uses your browser's local storage, not cookies:

NameWherePurposeKept until
admin_keyDashboardYour signed sign-in session (not your password)You sign out, or 7 days
current_event_idDashboardRemembers which event you were viewingYou clear browser data
scanner_keyDoor scannerThe gate PIN, so staff can reopen the scannerYou clear browser data
scanner_name, scanner_event_dayDoor scannerGate label and event dayYou clear browser data
stub_storage_notice_v1WebsiteRemembers that you dismissed this noticeYou clear browser data

On shared phones used for scanning, clear the browser's site data after the event to remove the saved gate PIN.

Cookies set by others

  • Cloudflare may set strictly necessary security cookies (such as __cf_bm) to protect the site from bots.
  • Razorpay sets cookies inside its payment window when you buy credits, to process and secure the payment. See Razorpay's own privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts doesn't set cookies, but your browser contacts Google's servers to download fonts.

Your choices

You can clear or block site data in your browser settings. If you do, you'll be signed out, and the door scanner will ask for its PIN again.
