* 08 / QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Good questions.

Everything you need to know about setting up your festival passes, gate turnstiles, and zero-fee payouts.

How do you prevent WhatsApp screenshot sharing and scalpers? +
Every mobile pass generates a rolling cryptographic token that expires every 15 seconds, paired with an active optical watermark frequency sweep. When scanned at the gate, screenshots of passes are automatically rejected by our edge turnstile algorithm.
What happens if the festival venue loses internet connection? +
The scanner application runs completely offline using IndexedDB local storage and WebAssembly HMAC cryptography. Bouncers can continue scanning passes at full speed without Wi-Fi or cellular service. Scans automatically reconcile once connectivity resumes.
Can attendees add passes directly to Apple Wallet and Google Wallet? +
Yes. Every pass is bundled with native PKPass files for Apple Wallet and Google Wallet with automated lockscreen geolocation triggers when attendees arrive near the venue.
Why is there zero ticketing commission? +
TicketScanner is built on serverless edge infrastructure (Cloudflare D1 & Workers), which costs fractions of a cent per scan to operate. We pass the savings to organizers with a flat ₹2.50 per pass instead of taking a 10% slice of your ticket sales.
