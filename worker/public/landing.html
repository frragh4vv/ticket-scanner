05 — Questions

Before your first event.

Anything else? Get in touch.

Do attendees need an app?

No. Each attendee gets their pass by email and shows the QR on their phone or a printout. Door staff scan with their phone's browser too: no app install, just the gate link and PIN you share.

Can someone get in twice with a screenshot?

No. Every scan is checked live against one shared record across all your gates. The second time a pass is scanned it shows "Already used" with the time, unless you choose to allow re-entry for that day.

Does scanning work without internet?

No, on purpose. If phones checked passes offline, two gates could each admit the same screenshot. Each scanning phone needs a connection; a mobile hotspot per gate is enough for most venues.

Can I run multi-day festivals?

Yes. Passes can cover up to 10 days, or only some of them. Scanners are set to the current day, and a pass used on the wrong day is flagged.

How do I collect money for tickets?

Two ways. Sell online through your own Razorpay account: buyers pay on your TixDesk booking page, the money goes straight to you, and passes are emailed as soon as payment clears. Or collect your way (UPI, cash, a form, another ticketing site) and issue passes yourself. Either way TixDesk takes no commission on ticket sales. You only use one credit per pass.

What is a credit, and do they expire?

One credit makes one pass. You start with 50 free, and the setup tour makes a few sample passes on us. After that it's ₹10 a pass, ₹8 at 2,500 and ₹5 at 10,000. Credits never expire, and unused packs are refundable within 7 days.

How do I put names on passes?

Generate blank passes first, then paste your list (or upload a spreadsheet) as "Name, email". You see exactly who gets which pass before anything is saved. Filling in names is free; only generating uses credits.

Can I have VIP, General and Guestlist?

Yes. Turn on tiers when you create the event. Each pass prints its tier and you can count and filter check-ins by tier.

What if an email doesn't arrive?

Ask the guest to check spam, then resend from the dashboard in one click. You can also download any pass as an image or PDF and send it on WhatsApp.

What do gate staff see?

Only the scanner. The event PIN unlocks scanning for that one event; staff can't see your guest list, emails or settings.

What happens to attendee data?

It's used only to issue, send and check passes. You can export or delete it any time, and we never sell it or use it for ads. Read the Privacy Policy.
